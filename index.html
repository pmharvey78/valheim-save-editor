ᚠ Valheim Item Level Editor

Raise the level of weapons, armour and shields in a Valheim character file, or add and remove inventory items. Fan-made and unofficial: not affiliated with or endorsed by Iron Gate or Coffee Stain. Runs entirely in your browser — your file is never uploaded anywhere.

Before you start

  • Back up your character file first. Copy it somewhere safe. If anything goes wrong, put the copy back.
  • Fully quit Valheim (not just to the main menu) before replacing the file, or the game will overwrite your edit.
  • Intended for single-player / your own worlds, at your own risk. Servers running anti-cheat mods may delete items they think are too high level.
  • Items whose total damage goes over 10,000 get flagged as cheated by the game. The editor warns you when that would happen.
  • Adding items is new and not yet verified in game. Added items are written in the same format the game uses and checked by two independent parsers, but nobody has loaded one in Valheim yet — keep your backup. Servers running anti-cheat mods may remove added items.
  • Levels are capped at 100 here. Anything far above the normal maximum is untested; very high levels are not recommended.
Where is my character file?
  • Steam Cloud saves (the default): C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\userdata\<your id>\892970\remote\characters\
  • Local saves: %userprofile%\AppData\LocalLow\IronGate\Valheim\characters_local\
  • The file is named after your character in lowercase, e.g. ragnar.fch. Ignore the .fch.old backups.
  • After editing, replace the original file with the downloaded one (same name). If Steam later reports a cloud sync conflict, choose to keep the local files.
